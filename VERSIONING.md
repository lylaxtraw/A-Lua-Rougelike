## Version 0.1.0
* Base files for the game to be playable
* VERY prone to have bugs, issues, crashes or inconsistencies
* Beta builds only available for macOS & Windows
* Some concept sprites and sfx included but not implemeted to the game yet
* Knonw bugs:
    * Floor 5's Slime is more likely to be a Heavy for some reason
    * Some flavour text is not pathhed correctly, so you'll see stuff like {POTION_DRANK}
    * Charms are in early testing, and may not be unlocked after winning a run or get permastuck behind impossible checks
    or not even work
    * Insanity charm's code isn't up to date with it's description, so it'll just do it's first effect (multi charms)
    * All Slime combos are in testing, so they may be 1 or 2 combos that are unbeatable due to their mechanics clashing
    * Trying to overrwrite a save may crash the ap and/or delete the save and all stored data
    * Status may stack in way they aren't meant to if they're applied in a certain order
* General tips (see NOTES.pdf for more fleshed out info):
    * Shop items are random every campfire
    * Only the base Slime has the Anger Point ability, so go full ham on the other 2
    * If Anger Point triggers more than once in an early floor, focus on healing yourself
    * Mana potions are more recommended than heatlh potions
    * Spells don't have their type shown during gameplay, the names are your only hint
    * Have atleast 3 different type spells before reaching your first Ruler to avoid a possible softlock
    * Have a high tier weapon and shield before facing a Master (high tier weapons cost +10k)
    * There's no way to 100% predict the next slime, apart from when it's going to be a Elite, Ruler, or Master
