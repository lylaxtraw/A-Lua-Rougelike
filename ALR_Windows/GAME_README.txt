- To open the game:
  Extract the downloaded ZIP folder completely, then double-click "A-Lua-Rougelike.exe".
  * Windows Defender / SmartScreen Note:
    If Windows displays "Windows protected your PC" (Unrecognized App):
    Click "More info" ("Más información") -> Click "Run anyway" ("Ejecutar de todas formas").

- Feedback & Notes:
  There's a 'betatest_notes.txt' file for you to take notes. Please be as expansive, critical, and honest as possible when writing! This helps me improve and know what my audience truly wants!

- Documentation:
  Check 'NOTES.pdf' for the full gameplay mechanics, elemental chart, and combat guide!

- Save Files:
  Your save data is stored at:
  %appdata%\LOVE\ (or %appdata%\A-Lua-Rougelike\)
  Press [Win + R], type %appdata% and press Enter to open this folder.
  Please don't touch it unless the game failed to delete a dead run or a bug corrupted your save.
  (If you REALLY want to, you CAN edit it to make yourself OP, but I strongly suggest trying to beat the game normally first!)

- Pro Tips:
  * Don't forget to equip your weapons & shields at the campfire! Otherwise you are more likely to lose!
  * It's highly recommended to ONLY buy spells you CAN use. The spellbook is currently not optimized to handle all spells being bought, so you can only choose up to 9 spells!
  * Chances are you're gonna have a hard time beating the first Elite Slime, since it's more likely to be a Heavy Slime. Try and focus on potions!!

Good luck on your climb to wave 100!
